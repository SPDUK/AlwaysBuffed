# Forever consumable rankings

The presets use Forever talent trees from client build `1.60.1.70959` and Blizzard's class deep dives and hotfixes. The effects below use the catalog's spell data. The relative weights are AlwaysBuffed's leveling and dungeon estimates. Equipment, hit caps, target resistance, combat length, and actual talent ranks can change an individual choice. [Catalog sources](catalog.md) · [Blizzard's Forever hotfix log](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-16-2026)

**Best for your spec** covers direct damage, healing, or mitigation for the chosen role. **Utility** covers situational effects, relevant resource sustain, and defense for non-tanks. Food that grants kill XP keeps utility while leveling, even if the other stat has no value to the build. At level 60 the same food is evaluated on its remaining stats. **Not useful** covers the effects that contribute neither build value nor utility. An item can belong to any group and still be locked by level, skill, ownership, weapon type, or spell knowledge.

The default shopping and bag choices prefer direct effects, then useful utility. Lowest price considers useful purchasable choices with a known price. Tracking a specific item gives it priority when it is usable. Plain recovery food stays available as the last automatic bag fallback. Rogue poisons, Mage Comprehension scrolls, and other weapon effects also use the item and enchant rules for the equipped hand.

## Warrior

Weaponmaster and Bloodthrill support weapon-based Arms damage. Fury's Flurry and Dual Wield Specialization raise the relative value of crit, speed, and accuracy. Protection emphasizes surviving hits through shield and mitigation talents while retaining useful threat stats. ## Hunter

Beast Mastery uses pet talents alongside the Hunter's ranged attacks. Marksmanship's Careful Aim converts Intellect to Attack Power, and Mortal Shots rewards critical hits. Survival's Savage Strikes, Strider Kick, and Lightning Reflexes favor melee hits, weapon effects, and Agility. This preset models melee-focused Survival. Mana-only buffs do not earn a place in its usual solo buff choices. [Blizzard class deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301515/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-hunter-and-druid) · [Everloot Hunter trees](https://everloot.app/tools/talents/hunter)

## Mage

Frost's Shatter and Fingers of Frost, Arcane's Arcane Blast and Arcane Mind, and Fire's Ignite and Heating Up inform each school preference. Generic spell damage works for all three. Comprehension's damage procs count toward direct damage, with lower weight for cooldown-limited expected damage. A weapon hit that restores mana, a freeze proc, and resistance penetration remain situational. ## Druid

Forever lets weapons and enchants contribute damage in Cat and Bear forms. Cat favors damage and critical hits through Shred and Primal Bite. Bear values armor and Stamina through its mitigation role and talents such as Thick Hide and Natural Reaction. Balance uses Nature and Arcane through Wrath, Moonfire, and Eclipse. Restoration prioritizes healing and sustain through Wild Growth, Living Spirit, and Reflection. Select Bear manually to favor tanking within the shared Feral Combat tree. [Blizzard class deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301515/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-hunter-and-druid) · [Everloot Druid trees](https://everloot.app/tools/talents/druid)
