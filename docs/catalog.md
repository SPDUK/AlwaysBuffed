# Forever consumable catalog

The catalog targets World of Warcraft: Forever build `1.60.1.70959`. It has 358 distinct item and spell IDs across 34 categories for levels 1–60. The default Combat bar covers food, elixirs and flasks, scrolls, weapon enhancements and imbues, quest rewards, utility draughts, campfires, the Cozy Sleeping Bag, and Herbalism Tea. Four Fishing categories and one Mage Comprehension category have dedicated bars. The spec presets cover nine classes and all 27 talent groups, with a separate Feral Bear preset. [Profession buff evidence](profession-buffs.md)

## How buffs are grouped

| Layer | Choices and combinations |
| --- | --- |
| Well Fed | Track one cooked food at a time. Most Forever recipes apply their 15-minute stat and 5% kill experience buff after ten seconds of eating. Basic recovery food can fill an empty bag choice. |
| Extra food | Dragonbreath Chili applies a separate ten-minute melee fire proc. Festival treats are carried-only alternatives. |
| Elixirs and flasks | Offensive, defensive and regeneration choices use separate recommendation groups. Attack Power Draught groups the two Firewaters with Juju Might because they grant overlapping attack power. Same-stat elixirs can compete. A category does not certify that every combination of buffs will stack. |
| Scrolls and familiars | Six stat scroll families each have four ranks. A matching class or camp buff may overlap with a stat scroll. Mages can separately track three familiar scrolls with hour-long Intellect effects. |
| Temporary weapon enchantment | Each equipped melee hand has one selector for a compatible sharpening stone, weightstone or oil. Sharp and blunt restrictions come from the client spell's weapon subclass mask. |
| Class imbue | Each equipped melee hand can use an imbue alongside its stone or oil. Rogues have five poison families, Shaman have their four imbue spells, Warlocks have conjured Firestones and Spellstones, and Mages have Comprehension weapon scrolls. Mage scrolls can require a staff, dagger or sword. |
| Quest rewards and situational buffs | Blasted Lands draughts share a one-hour cooldown. Winterspring Juju Power and Guile and Un'Goro Crystal Force and Ward appear alongside related stat items. Crystal Spire, resistance draughts, Gift of Arthas, water mobility and detection are optional categories. |
| Camp and rest | Three campfire kits require Cooking 1, 90 or 220. Camp Benefits and Well-Rested each have their own reminder. |
| Fishing | Pole lures, Well Fed fishing skill, the Shiny Silver Coin, and catch-quality effects from a Fishing Rack or Hut have their own selectors on the Fishing bar. |
| Gathering and research | Herbalism Tea tracks Goldthorn Tea on the default Combat bar. Mages can switch to Comprehension to track Scroll of Comprehension. |

The five Blasted Lands quest draughts use the same item cooldown category `103` with a one-hour recovery. The Un'Goro pylon rewards appear in the client data for Crystal Force, Ward and Spire. Instant and Deadly values are base damage inputs and actual damage depends on the proc rate and target. ## Campfire and sleeping bag auras

| Client aura | Reminder behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Camp Benefits `1229741` | Active after one minute sitting or crafting at a camp with contributed benefits. The normal duration is one hour. Violet Dust extends the same aura to two hours for anyone who rests at that fire. The addon identifies that variant from the reported total aura duration and uses the actual expiry for both. |
| Welcoming Campfire `1289723` and `1229739` | The build has two one-minute variants that both trigger Camp Benefits. The reminder shows this stage as settling in. |
| Campfire Nearby `1283391`, Boosted Rest `1229451`, and Violet Flames `1328362` | Camp proximity, the Camp Tent's rested experience cooldown, and the purple fire visual, respectively. |
| Well-Rested `429959` | The completed sleeping bag buff lasts two hours and adds 1% experience per minute of resting, up to three stacks. Parties can share a sleeping bag. Using your own item requires it in your bags and has an hour-long cooldown. |
| Well-Rested Tracking Aura `429960` and Well Rested `1225478` | The first is an untimed internal trigger for `429959`. The second increases rested XP accrual and cap. |

The reminder reads the player's aura by spell ID and displays its localized name, icon and expiration. A shared camp or sleeping bag can provide an active reminder even when the character has no corresponding item to use. ## Availability and scope

Item records establish effects, requirements and cooldowns. Quest rewards, vendor stock, phased raid content and hotfixes can depend on the server. Quest rewards and legacy event items are carried-only choices. Some client rows have unavailable or unverified acquisition, including the legacy Instant Toxin recipe, old Season of Discovery poisons and raid-phase Zanza supplies.

The upkeep wheel covers lasting buffs for the active loadout. Health and mana potions, oils that act as short combat cooldowns, explosives, encounter resist absorbs, Juju Flurry and Escape, and Un'Goro Crystal Charge, Restore and Yield are situational combat tools. Profession coverage is limited to the effects listed above, including the two fishing camp features with catch bonuses.
